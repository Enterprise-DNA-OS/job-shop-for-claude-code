description: The chasing letter, drafted from the record into drafts/. Payment claims chase differently from plain invoices. Never sent from here.
---

1. Read the position first: `debtors`, then `customer <name>` for the history, the notes and anything disputed.
2. Draft to `drafts/overdue-<number>.md` in the operator's voice, short and factual: the invoice, the amount, the due date, the days, what happens next.
3. On a payment claim, say so: no payment schedule arrived by the due date, so the claimed amount is payable in full under the Construction Contracts Act 2002 and recoverable as a debt. That sentence does more than three reminders.
4. On a plain invoice, the tone is the relationship's: read the notes first. A customer disputing two hours gets the time record attached, not a threat.
5. The operator sends it. Log the send.
