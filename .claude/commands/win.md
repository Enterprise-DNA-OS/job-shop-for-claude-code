---
description: A quote landed. Won, promised, onto the floor, with the record straight from the first hour.
---

1. `job won <ref> --po=<their PO> --promised=<date>`. No promise date means late can never be measured: push for one.
2. `job start <ref>` when it actually hits the floor, not before. The gap between won and started is real information about the backlog.
3. From the first hour: `time <ref> <person> <hours> --centre=<code>` and `issue <ref> <item> <qty> [--heat=]` as they happen. The costing is only as honest as the floor is.
4. On a structural job the heat number gate is not optional: the number is on the mill certificate that came with the steel. If the cert turns up later, `heat <ref> <item> <number>` backfills it honestly.
5. Material not on the shelf: `po raise <supplier> [--job=<ref>]`, `po add`, and watch it on `po`. A job-linked PO lands its cost on the job at receipt.
