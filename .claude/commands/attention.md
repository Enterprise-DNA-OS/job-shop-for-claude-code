---
description: Everything that wants a decision, worst first. Missing heat numbers on structural steel, overdue services, quotes being eaten, late promises, shortages, money asleep.
---

1. Run `npm run shop -- attention`.
2. The list is already ordered by how much each item can cost. Read it in that order and do not reorder it by ease:
   - **A traceable item on a structural job with no heat number** outranks everything. When the engineer or the certifier asks, the paperwork answers or the job does not.
   - **A work centre past its service** is a HSWA duty already breached; book it and record it.
   - **A fixed quote 80% eaten on an unfinished job** is margin bleeding in real time. Variation, recover, or eat it knowingly; the one wrong answer is not deciding.
   - **Late promises, shortages and overdue POs** are the customer conversation you want to start, not receive.
   - **Finished-not-billed, overdue invoices, drafts never sent, retentions due** are the company's own money, asleep.
   - **Over-limit exposure and five figures owing with no PPSR** are how a customer's bad year becomes yours.
   - **Timesheet gaps, cold quotes and stale tasks** close the list, but a cold quote is revenue walking away.
3. For each item, say the one action: the command to run, the call to make, or the record to fix. Name who owns it.
4. Anything that needs a letter or an email is drafted, never sent: `npm run docs`, or write to `drafts/`.

If the operator asks "what should I do today", pick the top three and say why those three.
