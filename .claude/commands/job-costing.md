---
description: What every job really cost against what it billed. Materials, hours, outwork, margin. The re-quoting conversation starts here.
---

1. Run `npm run shop -- costing` (open jobs) or `costing --all` (closed included).
2. Read from the bottom of the margin column up: the money-losers first. For each, say why in one line: hours past the estimate, materials re-priced since the quote, outwork nobody allowed for.
3. The fix is never editing the numbers. It is a variation on the live job, or a better number on the next quote for that family of work.
4. One job's full breakdown: `npm run docs -- job-cost-report` renders it on the letterhead.
