---
description: One account in full, or the list ordered by exposure. Owing plus WIP against the limit, the PPSR position, jobs, notes and open tasks.
---

1. One account: `npm run shop -- customer <name>`. The list: `customers`.
2. Exposure is owing plus the WIP on their open jobs. Over the limit means no new quotes without the operator's own decision; the stop is `stop <customer>` and it writes itself into the record.
3. Five figures owing with no PPSR registration is on `attention`: retention-of-title terms are worth nothing unregistered (PPSA 1999). When it is done at ppsr.govt.nz: `ppsr <customer> --registered=`.
4. Before drafting anything for a customer, read their whole card: the jobs, the notes, the money. The notes are where the disputes were already answered once.
