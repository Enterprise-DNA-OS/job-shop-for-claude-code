---
description: Conversations, floor events and decisions onto the record. The disputes and the follow-ups both read it.
---

1. `npm run shop -- log <ref|customer> "what happened" [--channel=phone|email|floor|site|counter] [--by=] [--on=]`.
2. Log at the moment it happens: the variation agreed on the phone, the "go quiet" after a disputed hour, the promise made at the counter. Two weeks later nobody can argue with a dated note.
3. A note that implies work becomes a task: `task add "..." --customer= --due=`.
4. Quotes going cold are measured off this record: a chase call that is not logged did not happen.
