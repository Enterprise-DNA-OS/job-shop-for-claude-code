description: One job in full. The plan against what was issued, the hours, the purchase orders, the invoices and the notes, with the cost and margin position.
---

1. Run `npm run shop -- job <ref>` (ref, customer name or a word from the title all work).
2. Present the story in order: what was promised and when, what it has cost so far (materials, labour, outwork), what it is worth, what has been billed, what is left.
3. Watch for and say out loud: issues missing heat numbers on a structural job, planned material never issued (the costing is lying), a fixed quote nearly eaten, an overdue PO holding the job, and hours from people whose rates look wrong.
4. Changes go through the commands: `plan`, `issue`, `time`, `job done`, `bill`, never by editing rows.
